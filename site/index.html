Margin is a free space mission design game. Build a small Earth observation satellite, predict what will hold it back, launch it, fly it for a month and read exactly why it ended the way it did.

Real orbits. A real Earth.

The numbers behind the game are sourced, not made up for it. Simplified teaching values are labelled as such.

  • NASA GMAT

    The General Mission Analysis Tool computes every orbit and every ground station pass the game uses, from Svalbard to Wallops.

  • Blue Marble & Black Marble

    NASA Earth Observatory imagery wraps the 3D Earth: the day side, and the city lights on the night side.

  • Every value has a source

    Parts, launchers and stations each carry a source and a provenance label in the game data.
